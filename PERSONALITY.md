# CivicShift personality

## Brand idea

CivicShift helps students turn free time into accountable community impact. It should feel optimistic and capable, never childish or bureaucratic.

## Voice

- Clear before clever
- Encouraging without exaggeration
- Direct about status, requirements, and errors
- Protective of young volunteers' privacy
- Respectful of nonprofits' limited time

## Writing patterns

- Use short, active headings: “Protect your time.” “Every hour, accounted for.”
- Explain why a field or rule exists when it affects eligibility or privacy.
- Pair errors with a specific next step.
- Label seeded content and unfinished external integrations honestly.
- Avoid invented impact claims, testimonials, and real-person information.

## Visual character

- Manrope for friendly, highly readable interface typography
- Deep navy for trust and structure
- Electric blue for primary actions
- Coral for urgency and human warmth
- Yellow for achievement and attention
- Mint for verified, safe, and completed states

## Accessibility

Use semantic landmarks, visible focus, keyboard-friendly controls, high contrast, descriptive labels, responsive layouts, reduced-motion support, and plain-language error messages throughout.
